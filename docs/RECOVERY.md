# Recovery matrix

What happens when collection or the speed ramp fails, what recovers on its own, what needs a person, and which log line proves it.
Written from the code at this branch plus the logging (`feat/oct4-logging`), stale-lease (`feat/oct4-w2-collection`) and alert (`feat/oct4-w2-health`) branches.
Where a row names an event from those branches it exists only after they are merged. Ramp events (`ramp.*`) are written by this branch either through `server/obs.py` (if present and active) or as the same JSON line into `data/logs/events.jsonl`; `ops/diagnose --tail 60` (logging branch) shows them, and so does `ops/set-pace --ramp status`.

Rule for every automatic recovery: **the ramp comes back one level lower than it was, never from the top**, and a warning, challenge or login is never recovered automatically.

| # | Failure | Recovers by itself (and after how long) | Needs a person: exact action | Log event that proves it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **429 or soft block** | The extension rests that request kind 10 min, doubling per hit within 24 h up to 6 h; 3 hits in 24 h = at least 2 h (core.js). The server sets a shared cooldown of 15 min or Retry-After. Ramp: back to the previous level at once, holds 2 clean windows (about 4 h), then climbs. If the server also paused the stages (only while a warning isolation is active), the ramp never restarts warning isolation. Ordinary protective stops may be restarted only when automatic recovery is separately enabled and an exact server-recorded cause proves which stages and identities were running. It waits 2 h and preserves those stages, at most twice in 24 h. | A second limit in 24 h stops the ramp: `ops/set-pace --ramp resume --account @name` once you are happy. A third automatic restart in a day is refused the same way. | `ig.adverse` (code rate_limit / soft_block), `ramp.step_down` (kind, http_status, to_pages_per_hour, hold_windows), `ramp.auto_resume` (rested_min), `ramp.auto_resume_refused` |
| 2 | **Followers home redirect** | The server rests 30 min, the followers route 2 h (4 h if twice in 6 h); following lists keep running. Ramp: sees it (`redirect_seen`), may restart only an explicitly recorded ordinary protective pause after 2 h when automatic recovery is separately enabled (`auto_resume`, at most twice a day), then the first pages after the gap step down one level. Two redirects in one window: step down and hold. With the opt-in `follower_redirect_recovery=route` the server recovers without pausing. | If it keeps recurring (auto restarts used up): look at the list, then `ops/set-pace --ramp resume`. | `ig.adverse` (reason list_html_home_redirect), `ramp.redirect_seen`, `ramp.auto_resume` (code home_redirect), `ramp.step_down` (kind outage or redirects) |
| 3 | **Unconfirmed request / lease expiry** | Jobs: a lease with no result expires after 10 min and the job is requeued (repair step; w2-collection also cleans stale leases). The request itself is not recovered: it may have reached Instagram, so both stages stay paused. Ramp: stops and steps down one level. | Open the workspace banner "A request has no confirmed end", check the account in Instagram, confirm (control action `review_unconfirmed_request`), start collection, then `ops/set-pace --ramp resume --account @name`. | `pipeline_events request_expired_unknown`, `state.setting instagram_request_attention`, `ramp.step_down` (kind stop), `ramp.resume` |
| 4 | **Extension offline / Chrome closed** | Account is released after 10 min offline and its list moves to another lane. If browser startup is configured, Chrome profiles reopen at server start or on resume (`browser_startup`). When pages return after a gap over 30 min the ramp resumes one level lower with a fresh window. A window with under 90% runtime never counts. | If Chrome does not come back: open the profile (a banner appears after 30 min offline with w2-health). Nothing for the ramp. | `ext.heartbeat_gap` / `heartbeat_silent` / `heartbeat_back`, `ramp.step_down` (kind outage, gap_min) or `ramp.hold` (runtime share) |
| 5 | **Service worker restart** | Chrome restarts the worker from an alarm or message; pace, cooldowns, counters and outbox live in extension storage; the pending outbox replays (simulator: stopped mid-request and after commit, no page lost or doubled). The pace stays the stepped one (`paceCfg`). Gaps under 20 min are not an outage for the ramp. | None. | `ext` events from the extension log batch, `ramp.*` unchanged |
| 6 | **Server crash or restart** | LaunchAgent `com.fortunate.leads` has `KeepAlive` true, 10 s throttle, so it restarts in about 10 s; the extension retries its outbox. Ramp state is in the database; on the first tick after an absence over 30 min the window restarts (`server_gap`). The pace in force is stored too, so nothing resets. | If it crash-loops: `ops/diagnose` (crash dump in `data/crash/`). | `proc.start`, `proc.previous_run` (clean / crashed / killed), `ramp.server_gap` |
| 7 | **Mac sleep or reboot** | After sleep everything resumes; the gap (over 30 min) makes the ramp resume one level lower. After reboot the LaunchAgent starts at login (RunAtLoad); Chrome profiles reopen only if browser startup is configured, and the ramp resumes one level lower when pages return. | Open the Chrome profiles if startup is not configured. | `proc.slept` (logging), `proc.start`, `ramp.step_down` (kind outage) |
| 8 | **Scraping warning, challenge, login** | **Nothing, by design.** The server and extension stop every account; the ramp stops and steps down one level and never auto-restarts collection. | Read `docs/INSTAGRAM_WARNING_RECOVERY.md`: review in Instagram, acknowledge in the workspace, resume selected accounts, then `ops/set-pace --ramp resume`. | `ig.adverse` (challenge/login), `state.setting instagram_scraping_warning`, `ramp.step_down` or `ramp.step_down_none` (kind stop; the second when already at the starting pace) |
| 9 | **Disk low** | The ramp freezes (no step) while less than 2 GB is free and continues on its own once there is room. The server's own writes can fail first: `ramp.tick_failed` is logged and the worker keeps going. | Free space (`ops/storage_cleanup.py` dry-run, `ops/storage.md`). | health `disk_low` / `disk_full` (w2-health alert), `ramp.freeze` (reason mentions the disk), `ramp.tick_failed` |
| 10 | **A ramp step that made things worse** | Rate below 90% of before the step: undone at the next window, hold 2 windows. Response time up in two windows in a row: one level down, hold 2. Response time up once: hold only. Pace changed by hand: the ramp stands down and never overrides. | `ops/set-pace --ramp off --restore` to go back to the original pace entirely. | `ramp.step_down` (before, pages_per_hour, previous_overhead_s), `ramp.hold`, `ramp.stand_down`, `ramp.restore` |

## Reading the log after a failure

```sh
ops/set-pace --ramp status      # the last decisions, the numbers behind them, and who must act
ops/diagnose --since 6h         # (logging branch) server, extension, Instagram events; ramp events are component "ramp"
grep '"component":"ramp"' data/logs/events.jsonl | tail -20
```

Every ramp event carries: `lane`, `account`, `reason` (a sentence), `ramp_level`, `mode`, and the measured numbers
(`pages`, `pages_per_hour`, `runtime_share`, `median_gap_s`, `overhead_s`, `outages`, `target`, `expected`, `to_id`).

## Restart permission

Automatic stage restart defaults off. Saved adverse events or two paused stages are never enough evidence to restart. The server records a single-use `ramp_protective_stop` with the exact cause, lane, time, pre-stop stages and account identities before a protective pause. Any operator control invalidates it, including Stop pressed while already paused. An active request, warning, isolation, hold, stale heartbeat or changed identity blocks recovery. A lists-only run leaves bios paused after recovery. Existing pacing floors, cooldowns, budgets and personal-account protections remain in force.
