# Fortunate Leads handoff

Updated 1 October 2026. This file describes the current implementation. Earlier iterations and their validation remain in Git history.

## Current product

The connection map keeps Michael at the centre of an irregular circular arrangement of photos. The toolbar separates two choices: Size by (Followers, Fit, Shared sources or Equal) and Show (recorded follow direction). Changing size repacks the same people without changing the camera or their evidence order. Follower sizes use a compressed scale so large accounts remain readable alongside small ones. The owner is 24 pixels across at the default zoom. Dragging a photo temporarily moves it, then returns it to its recorded position; dragging empty space pans the map. Photos have no outlines or overlaid status/source dots, including on hover and selection. Recorded follow direction remains available through Show filters and the selected person’s details. A follow or shared source does not establish friendship or an introduction.

Zoom magnifies the same people. Explicit pages offer 250, 500 or 1,000 people, default 500. Search and filters reach other people without zoom gating. The selected card provides status and existing manual tags directly. Add tag opens a small editor; connection facts sit under Connections. Esc closes the editor without discarding a draft. Saved photos queue from the centre outward, with the owner first. Loading stays limited to six concurrent requests and a 1,024-entry cache. Request order is radial; completion can vary with photo response times. A full saved-data walk verified all 111,698 non-owner people across 112 pages, without duplicates or omissions. Median HTTP page time was 19.13 ms, maximum 70.76 ms. See [measurements and limits](MAP_VIEW_PERFORMANCE.md).

The UI uses neutral greys. Get started is removed; setup belongs in Accounts. Collection has one Start/Stop control, with Connect accounts when an enabled account is offline. Running means an active request permit, not merely an assigned job. The summary distinguishes new profiles, saved list entries and successful bio reads; today uses UTC midnight. Protected main accounts do not hide an alternate account’s daily cap or offline state. Requests used are labelled separately from successful saved results. ETA covers only the known current queue, uses distinct newly saved entries, and shows Waiting for saved pages until there is evidence for a pace estimate. Optional suggestions and AI setup are collapsed. Local AI startup belongs in Settings; Accounts stays focused on collection. Review shows two connection lines with the rest expandable and puts supporting evidence behind a disclosure. Lead rows retain recorded connection direction at laptop widths; opening details temporarily makes room by closing filters and restores them afterward. Qualification remains separately controllable; rules-only collection does not activate AI scoring.

The Instagram Inbox shortcut opens the verified main account through the extension. It does not import or send messages. Optional message-history import retains metadata only.

The UI shows Connecting until a fresh matching extension heartbeat arrives, then revalidates the original resume command. Connect accounts reopens saved profiles without changing collection intent. Stop cancels a pending connection attempt; stale workers cannot resume it later. A connection attempt expires after 90 seconds and reports failure, without treating Chrome dispatch as success. Stopped collection and paused accounts remain stopped. No repeated watchdog reopens Chrome after a manual close. See [configuration](../ops/README.md).
